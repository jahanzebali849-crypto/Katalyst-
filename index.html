Who we are

The Katalyst started in 2023 with just six members and one clear goal: to help businesses achieve their goals. We cover finance, people, stock, marketing, research and quality, so every client gets one joined-up plan built from their own data. Our early clients trusted us with their real numbers, and that trust helped us grow. In 2025 we went global, and today we help businesses all over the world.

Why clients choose us

  • We read all your numbers and find the conflicts others would miss.
  • Every store or site gets its own plan, based on its own customers.
  • Your staff gain from the plan through commission and training.
  • We spend the budget in steps and stop what does not work.
  • Quality Assurance reports results to your board every week.
