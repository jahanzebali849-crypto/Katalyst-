Bake & Freeze at a glance

An appliance seller in Houston with North, South and West Houston stores, a Beaumont store and the East Houston warehouse. It sells Frigidaire and Kenmore ovens ($300–$400) and refrigerators ($350–$450), made in Mexico. Customers include Houston shoppers, Brazil and Mexico City.

West and South earn the most per square foot. The smallest store (West, 2,550 sq ft) earns more than three times as much per foot as Beaumont (5,000 sq ft), so space is not the problem. Customers and offers are. Profit on each item is strong ($200 to $300); the problem is volume.

Numbers and controlThe handout numbers conflict: profit fell 64% or about 80%; staff are 900 or 654.A plan built on wrong numbers can fail.
Money and profitProfit fell from about $60,000 to about $21,667 a month between April and June.Losses come from low volume, not low margins.

The profit drop: a normal month earns $60,000 profit. April was $24,000, May $21,500 and June $19,500, an average of $21,667: a 64% fall. The handout says about 80%. Our math gives 64%, and we flag the difference.

The plan, team member by team member

Sales and marketing
Bán Bercel

Use better sales measures, add a simple CRM and plan ads for each store: bilingual ads, social media, TikTok, Google and a new website. Build a clear B2B process. The $45,000 advertising budget: Spanish radio and TV $15,000, social media and search $15,000, local TV and print $10,000, community events $5,000.

Research and development
Unisha Koirala

Compare prices, brands, stock, warranties, delivery and service with rivals. Ask customers through surveys, reviews and sales check-ins. Research in month 1, pilots in months 2 to 3, review at month 6.

Finance
Kaue Caires

Audit payroll, set reorder points, cut fuel 15% and supplies 10%. Targets could save about $24,620 a year: $9,000 fuel, $11,000 supplies and $4,620 truck leases. They are targets, not promises.

Human relations
Haruka Takamiya

Replace the 3 + 3 daily quota with attainable store goals. Add a 4% sales commission (a $27K six-month pilot) and a monthly bonus for the top store. Bring back benefits as profit recovers, run sales training and hold monthly all-hands meetings.

Logistics
Christopher Guerrero

Confirm stock counts, move slow stock to West and South, order less from Mexico until stock nears 6 months, and set a weekly plan for the 7 trucks. Keep FedEx for Mexico, with air freight only for rush orders.

Quality assurance
Jawad Jumani

Check every figure before it reaches the client. Track sales, profit, people and stock for each store every month, and report weekly to the board.
